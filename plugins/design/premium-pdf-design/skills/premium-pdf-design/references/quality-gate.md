# Premium PDF quality gate

## Content

- Every factual claim is supplied, sourced, or clearly labeled as an assumption.
- Titles, names, dates, figures, citations, credits, and legal copy are consistent.
- Placeholder text and tool tokens are absent from the final export.
- The narrative, section order, and page purpose are clear.

## Design system

- Typography roles, margins, grid, spacing, colors, rules, image treatment, and folios are consistent.
- The publication has one recognizable art direction and no competing decorative systems.
- Body text, captions, source notes, and links remain readable at intended size.
- Dense pages are balanced by hierarchy, not merely boxes.

## Page inspection

- No clipping, overlap, accidental crop, widow, orphan, stranded heading, broken glyph, or blank unintended page.
- Line breaks, image crops, baselines, borders, and page turns look deliberate.
- Images are sharp enough for their rendered size and have correct proportions.
- Tables, lists, and callouts fit without microscopic text.

## Navigation and accessibility

- Page numbers, contents references, bookmarks when supplied, and links resolve correctly.
- Reading order is logical in the editable source.
- Contrast, language, labels, and meaningful link text are appropriate.
- Reduced accessibility in the export format is disclosed rather than hidden.

## Production

- Page size, orientation, bleed, margins, and print settings match the request.
- Fonts and assets are embedded or packaged lawfully where required.
- Text can be extracted unless the design explicitly and justifiably uses outlined display art.
- The final PDF was regenerated from the delivered source, then every final page was rendered and inspected.
