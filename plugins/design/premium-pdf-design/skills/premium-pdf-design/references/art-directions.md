# Contextual art directions

Choose one family, then adapt it to the content and brand. These are starting systems, not templates.

## Quiet editorial

- Best for essays, cultural guides, books, interviews, and thoughtful brands.
- Use a warm neutral canvas, serif/sans contrast, generous margins, fine rules, and selective full-bleed imagery.
- Let typography and image sequencing create energy.

## Contemporary field guide

- Best for travel, food, sport, hobbies, and practical discovery.
- Use clear navigation, map or label cues, strong photography, compact fact blocks, and a subject-derived accent.
- Keep functional details easy to scan without turning every item into a card.

## Precise business

- Best for proposals, capabilities, strategy, case studies, and executive briefs.
- Use a disciplined grid, crisp sans typography, restrained data color, confident section dividers, and exact evidence labels.
- Avoid generic blue gradients, stock handshakes, invented metrics, and dense slide-like pages.

## Tactile studio

- Best for portfolios, lookbooks, craft, architecture, interiors, and premium products.
- Use material neutrals, intentional crops, subtle texture, strong whitespace, and one recurring graphic mark.
- Keep texture light enough for text and images to remain authoritative.

## Energetic culture

- Best for music, gaming, sport, festivals, youth culture, and launches.
- Use bolder scale contrast, a tighter palette, dynamic crops, and controlled type movement across page turns.
- Preserve reading order and avoid stacking several loud motifs.

## Warm human

- Best for community, wellness, education, personal stories, and service guides.
- Use approachable type, natural imagery, soft but accessible color, and clear explanatory hierarchy.
- Avoid childish illustration or excessive rounded containers unless the audience calls for them.

## Selection test

Before committing, answer:

- What should the reader feel?
- What must they understand or do?
- Which single visual gesture makes this publication recognizable?
- Which treatment would be inappropriate for the subject?
- Would the system still work with real long-form content and weak source imagery?
